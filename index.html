// how I sell

Diagnose before you demo.

Most reps present. I ask until I find the workflow the product actually has to change, and the constraint that stopped every vendor before me. The demo comes after that, or it does not come at all.

Win the evaluation. Don't route around it.

In an AI deal, the technical evaluation is where trust is won or lost. I live inside it: multi-stage validation, run against the incumbent, until the buyer's own engineers are the ones making the case for me.

Build the ROI for their board, not mine.

A number I believe in means nothing. I build the model with the buyer, sized to their real volumes, so it survives the meeting I am not invited to.

A near-churn account is a turnaround waiting.

When an account is ready to leave, the relationship is usually still intact. The value story is what broke. I would rather commit to a hard turnaround than forecast the loss.

Close it once, then make it repeatable.

The best deals become playbooks. I turn what worked into onboarding other sellers can run, which is how DevRev's AI-seller program ended up in my hands across three regions.
