// how I work

Most of my deals are slow and technical. The person who signs is rarely the hard part. The engineers who will actually use the thing are. So before I show anyone a demo, I try to really understand their workflow and what's broken about it.

In AI deals there's usually a real evaluation, where their team tests whether it holds up. I stay close to that instead of steering around it, and I build the ROI case together with them so it still makes sense when I'm not in the room. By the end, their own engineers are usually the ones making the argument internally.

I've talked accounts out of leaving by fixing the value instead of writing them off. And when something works, I turn it into a playbook other people can run. That's how I ended up building DevRev's AI-seller onboarding across three regions.
